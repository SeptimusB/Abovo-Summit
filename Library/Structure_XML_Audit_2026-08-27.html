High-confidence inconsistencies

1. Incorrect worksheet ownership for named ranges

  • ImpairmentDirect is assigned to Development Consol Options twice, but the workbook name refers to Development BP Assumptions: lines 5975–5976 and 7364–7365.
  • Rep_Annual_Non_Tenure_Specific_Expenditure is assigned to Stock Disposal Assumptions, but belongs to Non Tenure Capital Assumptions: lines 9722–9723.
  • Rep_Periodic_Non_Tenure_Specific_Expenditure has the same incorrect worksheet: lines 9761–9762.

2. Incomplete Other Current Asset expansion definition

The datasource declares RowsExpandBy=NRRI at line 16106 but has neither RowsExpandModel nor RowExpandByNR. DataManager derives operational expansion behaviour from RowsExpandModel, so this datasource is currently treated as non-expanding.

3. Contradictory expansion metadata

  • Other Fixed Asset: RowsExpandModel=NRRI at line 15946, but RowsExpandBy=NRCI at line 15951.
  • Housing Asset: RowsExpandModel=NRRI at line 15313, but RowsExpandBy=CC at line 15318.
  • RowsExpandBy is not represented in the current ISEDatasource serializer; RowsExpandModel is the operative value. The conflicting values are therefore stale or misleading metadata.

4. Repairs expansion master is absent from its local child definition

RowExpandByNR is StockCondCats at line 3204, but that named range is not an NRDSName anywhere within the Repairs & Maint Assumptions child. The workbook range exists and the custom multi-range rule may deliberately use an external master, but this is the only RowExpandByNR without a local datasource representation.
